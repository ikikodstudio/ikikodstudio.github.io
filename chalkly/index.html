Chalkly

A digital board for teachers. For Windows and Mac.

Chalkly is a digital board for teachers who teach from their own computer on a projector or a screen.

  • Pen and realistic chalk
  • Eraser (whole strokes or an area), select and move, undo
  • Lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses and triangles
  • Green, black, white, grid and lined boards, or one from your own picture
  • Text boxes in four clear fonts
  • Paste or drag and drop pictures
  • Pages, page turning from the keyboard, full screen, hide the menus with one key
  • Screen pen: draw over your presentation or any other app and take a screenshot
  • Export to PDF and PowerPoint (.pptx)
  • Each lesson in a single .chalkly file
  • After an unexpected shutdown, your lesson can be recovered
  • Interface in 11 languages: Türkçe, English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, Русский, العربية, 中文, 日本語
